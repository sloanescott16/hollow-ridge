How to play

Enemy shooters hide among the spots across the valley. You cannot see them.

Hold on the valley to lean out of cover. Drag to aim. Release on a spot to fire. Release anywhere else to duck back.

While you are out, the enemy aim bar fills. If it fills, you are hit. Duck before it does.

Watch for a glint of glass while you lean out. A miss marks the spot cold (blue: nobody here or next to it) or warm (orange: somebody is right next to it).

Each cover sees a different stretch. Grey spots are out of view. Tap a cover below to move. Moving uses your turn.

When you fire, they see your flash and will aim faster at that cover next time. Their shots chip your cover and their flash gives them away.
